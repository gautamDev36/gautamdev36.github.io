1. Data Collection & Privacy

ChaText is committed to your privacy. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any sensitive information typed via our keyboard or observed via Accessibility Services. This includes:

  • No logging of keystrokes, passwords, or credit card numbers.
  • No collection of personal identifiers, contacts, or private messages.
  • No data transmission to external servers or third parties (except for non-personal ad-related data via Google Play Services).

2. Accessibility Service Usage

This app uses Android Accessibility Services for the following strictly functional purposes:

  • Event Monitoring: Detecting when you are typing in an input field (via the "typeViewTextChanged" event) to display the Stylish Text Bubble.
  • Text Transformation: Retrieving the text you have typed to transform it into your selected style and automatically replacing it in the text field when you tap a style.

This service is never used for monitoring activity beyond these specific styling features.

3. Stylish Keyboard

Our custom keyboard operates entirely offline. It does not have internet access permissions, ensuring your keystrokes never leave your device.
